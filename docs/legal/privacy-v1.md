## 1. Who runs this site

This site is `xerhero.com`, and it is run by one person: Carlo Greblo. There is no company
behind it, no team and no support desk.

The way to reach the operator is [the report page](/report), which also prints a mailbox.
The mailbox is a second channel: anything arriving there is copied into a report record by
hand before anything is done about it.

Reports and questions are read and answered on a best-effort basis, in one person's own
time. There is no service level and none is offered.

## 2. Signing in with Google

Signing in is Google only, and it goes through **Clerk**, which is this site's
authentication provider. There is no password, no email sign-up and no other identity
provider.

Google returns the following to Clerk: a stable account identifier (`sub`), your email
address and whether Google has verified it, your name (`name`, `given_name`,
`family_name`), the URL of your Google profile picture (`picture`), and your locale. The
site asks Google for the **`openid`**, **`email`** and **`profile`** scopes and nothing
else; the last two are required by Clerk and cannot be narrowed further.

**This site stores none of it.** Its own database holds a Clerk user id and the Handle you
choose, and nothing else about you as a person. There is no column for your email address,
so there is nothing to leak, sell or hand over.

**Your Google name, email address and profile picture are never displayed anywhere on this
site, to anyone, including you.** The profile picture arrives with a scope that cannot be
dropped; the only thing keeping it off this site is that no page renders it.

The identity is used for four things and nothing else: signing you in; attaching your
uploads, upvotes and bookmarks to an account; letting the operator contact you about a
takedown affecting a programme you uploaded; and nothing beyond those. **Signing in with
Google is used only to sign you in.** There is no profiling, no advertising, no sale of
data and no sharing with anyone who is not listed in section 10.

What Google does with the sign-in itself is Google's, and it is governed by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. What this site stores about you

A list of columns is the honest form, so here are the columns.

| Stored | Where | Kept until |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A sign-in id issued by Clerk | this site's database | you delete your account |
| Your Handle | this site's database | retired permanently when you delete your account or rename; never reassigned to anyone |
| Programmes and revisions you upload, and their metadata | this site's database and file storage | published permanently unless withdrawn or removed |
| The Handle as it stood at each upload | on the programme row | permanently — it is the credit |
| Your upvotes | this site's database | kept if you delete your account, unlinked from you |
| Your bookmarks | this site's database | deleted with your account |
| Unfinished uploads | this site's database | deleted with your account; swept after 24 hours regardless |

And the negatives, stated explicitly because their absence is the point: **no IP address
log, no analytics profile, no device fingerprint, and no email address.**

## 4. Your Handle, and what other people see

Your **Handle** is the only name anyone else sees, and you choose it. It appears on the
programmes you publish, on your contributor page and on the leaderboard.

Vote totals are public. **Who voted is not** — that is visible to the operator only.
**Bookmarks are private throughout**: there is no bookmark count anywhere, and a bookmark
is never shown to anyone but you.

Renaming your Handle retires the old one permanently. Nobody can claim it afterwards,
including you. Programmes you have already published keep the Handle they were published
under, because that Handle is the credit and rewriting it would rewrite the attribution
someone else is relying on.

## 5. Programmes you upload are public and permanent

On this site, upload is publish. There is no private tier, no drafts and no unlisted
programmes.

Everything published here is licensed under CC-BY 4.0, and that grant is **irrevocable**.
Forks and copies taken while a programme was published survive anything you do afterwards.
Files are served publicly and are cached by a content delivery network.

Deleting your account does not unpublish your programmes. Withdrawing them does — and even
then, forks taken while they were published stay where they are.

## 6. If you are named in a programme someone else uploaded

`.xer` files carry more than a schedule. They carry resource names, activity notes,
free-text fields and the name of the user who exported the file. Files are published
exactly as uploaded and **nothing is removed from them**.

If you are named in a file someone else uploaded, you can ask for it to be removed:
[report it](/report). You do not need an account.

**The remedy is removal of the revision, not editing of the file.** There is no way to
take one name out and keep the programme. If the name appears across a series of
revisions, which is usual, the whole series and everything forked from it is removed.

Download URLs carry a header asking search engines not to index or archive them, and the
file host tells crawlers to stay out. That is a request to well-behaved crawlers, not a
barrier: anyone who has the URL can fetch the file.

## 7. Reporting a programme

Anyone can report a programme, with no account.

What the form stores is what you reported, the name as it appears in the file if that is
what you are reporting, the reason you gave, and the contact details you supply.

**Your contact details are deleted 90 days after the case is closed.** The rest of the
report is kept as the record of a decision that was made.

## 8. Deleting your account

The order, and the consequences, stated here rather than discovered at deletion time:

1. This site's rows go first. The account record is deleted, your bookmarks are deleted,
   your upvotes are kept but unlinked from you, and your Handle is retired.
2. Then Clerk deletes the Google identity.

Programmes you have already uploaded **stay published**, credited to a Handle that by then
belongs to nobody. To remove them as well, **withdraw them first and then delete the
account** — two acts, both available to you, in that order.

## 9. Cookies, analytics and third parties

**There is no analytics on this site, of any kind.** No Google Analytics, no Vercel Web
Analytics, no Plausible, no pixel and no beacon. There is no advertising, there are no
trackers, there are no third-party embeds, and no fonts are loaded from anyone else.

**Cookies: the Clerk session cookie only.** It is set when you sign in and it is strictly
necessary for staying signed in. **A signed-out visitor receives no cookie from this site
at all.**

Because nothing is set that needs consent, **there is no cookie banner and no consent
manager.**

Two other parties run in your browser, and both are named rather than glossed:
**Cloudflare Turnstile**, which runs when you submit the report form and on no other page;
and **Cloudflare**, which serves the published files.

The operator therefore has no traffic data. Adding any analytics later would require a new
version of this policy and of the terms.

## 10. Where your data is held, and who else touches it

- **Clerk** — the authentication provider. It holds the Google identity: your name, email
  address and profile picture.
- **Neon** — the database, which holds the rows listed in section 3.
- **Cloudflare R2 and Cloudflare's network** — file storage and delivery for published
  programmes.
- **Vercel** — hosting for the site itself.
- **Google** — sign-in.
- **GitHub** — the public source code repository, and the scheduled job that mails the
  operator when something on the site breaks.

No data is sold, and none is shared with anyone outside that list.

## 11. Your rights

You can ask what this site holds about you, and you can ask for it to be deleted. Both go
to [the report page](/report), because there is no other channel — the site holds no email
address for you, so there is no inbox that could recognise you.

What is held is the list in section 3, and it is short by design: a sign-in id and a
Handle. Everything else about your Google identity is held by Clerk, and Clerk's own
policy governs it.

A request to delete your account is handled in the order set out in section 8. A request
to remove personal data from a published `.xer` file is handled as a removal under section
6 and under the terms.

## 12. Changes to this policy

Every version of this policy stays published at its own URL, permanently.

Changes ship as a new numbered version alongside a new version of the terms — one number
covers both documents. The version in force when you accepted the terms at each upload is
recorded against that upload, and it is linked from it.

**Nothing is edited in place.** A correction to a shipped version is a new version.
